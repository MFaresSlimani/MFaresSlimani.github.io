Research & publications

  • PhD dissertation · Feb 2025 – now · University of Boumerdès

    Deep learning for retinal vessel segmentation

    Feature-refinement and attention architectures (FR-UNet, Attention U-Net) for micro-vessel segmentation in fundus photos, aimed at earlier detection of diabetic retinopathy. Reproduced and benchmarked at 0.815 F1 on CHASE_DB1.

    PyTorch · TorchVision · OpenCV

    A 3D avatar that turns Arabic phrases into Algerian Sign Language gestures, built to support deaf and hard-of-hearing communities.

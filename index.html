Research

PhD dissertation, University of Boumerdès, since February 2025

Deep learning for retinal vessel segmentation

Feature-refinement and attention architectures (FR-UNet, Attention U-Net) for segmenting micro-vessels in fundus photos, aimed at earlier detection of diabetic retinopathy. I reproduced and benchmarked FR-UNet at 0.815 F1 on the CHASE_DB1 dataset. The faint branches behind this home screen are a nod to it.

PyTorch, TorchVision, OpenCV

Conference paper, Nafath, Qatar, December 2024

French Touch

A fast-food place in Bab Ezzouar, Algiers. Waiters used to type orders into their phone’s notes app. Now they take them at the table, the cashier watches a live queue, and the owner gets the day in numbers.

Riverpod 3, go_router and Supabase Realtime. Android, plus web for the cashier’s PC. Built and tested with GitHub Actions.

Read the case study Download the APK

Optica

Optical stores constantly order lenses from each other. Optica turns that into a network: browse partner stores, build an order by prescription, and the supplier ticks off what it can deliver and prints a ticket.

GetX, Supabase, Hive and ESC/POS thermal printing. French, English, Arabic and Tamazight. 81 tests and 14 golden screens.

Read the case study See the source

Muslim Zakat

Zakat is only due on wealth that stays above the nisab for a full lunar year. Most apps are a calculator with a 2.5% button. This one models the year itself, in the Hijri calendar.

Flutter, GetX and a Hijri rules engine. Arabic and English. A working demo with simulated bank sync.

Read the case study Download the APK
